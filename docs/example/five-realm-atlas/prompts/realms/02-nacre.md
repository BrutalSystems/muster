## Your realm: Nacre Dominion (east)

The Nacre Dominion is an eastern chain of tidal cities governed through pearl
archives that encode contracts, lineages, and debts. Its water is receding far
faster than ordinary tides, leaving ships and neighborhoods stranded in salt
flats. Explore memory as property, the politics of displaced port communities,
and the Dominion's competing claims over the exposed road and the missing
caravan. Nacre's eclipse interpretation should be persuasive but self-serving.

Visual direction: deep teal, nacreous cream, coral, and tarnished gold; tide
marks, shells, canals, ledger grids, and elegant curves.

Document form: write the chapter as an archive ledger — numbered entries,
registered claims, and annotations by archivists. This form is yours alone; the
other three realms are using different ones.

### Your two canonical borders

1. **Asterfall-Nacre:** Asterfall trades stormglass lenses for Nacre
   navigational pearls. A joint caravan disappeared at the Pale Stair eleven
   days ago; each side suspects the other concealed a survivor.
2. **Nacre-Embersteppe:** The receding eastern shoreline has exposed an ancient
   salt road. Nacre claims it by imperial charter, while Embersteppe clans are
   sheltering people displaced from newly stranded port towns.
