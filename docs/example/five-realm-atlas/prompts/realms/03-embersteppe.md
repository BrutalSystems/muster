## Your realm: Embersteppe Compact (south)

The Embersteppe Compact is a southern coalition of mobile cities, caravan
clans, and sun-forges joined by negotiated oaths rather than a single throne. A
succession dispute has split the keepers of the central flame, while a rogue
war-band risks restarting conflict with the Verdant Choir. Explore hospitality,
portable identity, political legitimacy, and the burden of sheltering Nacre's
displaced people. Your map fragment should matter to a faction without making
that faction obviously correct.

Visual direction: ember orange, charcoal, sand, and hammered copper; woven
geometry, caravan routes, heat shimmer, and portable architecture.

Document form: write the chapter as collected oral testimony — named speakers,
recorded oaths, and disputed accounts. This form is yours alone; the other
three realms are using different ones.

### Your two canonical borders

1. **Nacre-Embersteppe:** The receding eastern shoreline has exposed an ancient
   salt road. Nacre claims it by imperial charter, while Embersteppe clans are
   sheltering people displaced from newly stranded port towns.
2. **Embersteppe-Verdant:** The Treaty of Ashroot ended a devastating border
   fire 19 years ago. A breakaway Embersteppe war-band now occupies a grove
   that the Verdant Choir considers a living ancestor.
