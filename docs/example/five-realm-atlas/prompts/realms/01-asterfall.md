## Your realm: Asterfall (north)

Asterfall is a northern realm of mountain observatories, suspended roads,
storm-powered mechanisms, and severe civic ritual. Its prized stormglass can
magnify distant objects but sometimes appears to show events a few seconds
before they happen. Its heir vanished during an unauthorized observation at the
highest tower. Explore who earns access to knowledge, what a culture built
around prediction fears, and how the vanished caravan and the fifth signal
light threaten public confidence.

Visual direction: midnight blue, cold silver, pale electric cyan; angular
peaks, brass instruments, star charts, and fractured lenses.

Document form: write the chapter as an observatory record — dated observations,
instrument readings, and civic notices. This form is yours alone; the other
three realms are using different ones.

### Your two canonical borders

1. **Asterfall-Nacre:** Asterfall trades stormglass lenses for Nacre
   navigational pearls. A joint caravan disappeared at the Pale Stair eleven
   days ago; each side suspects the other concealed a survivor.
2. **Verdant-Asterfall:** Their high-pass signal towers have begun showing an
   impossible fifth light. Both realms publicly call it a mechanical fault and
   privately send expeditions toward it.
