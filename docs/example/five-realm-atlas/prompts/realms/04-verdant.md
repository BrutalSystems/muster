## Your realm: Verdant Choir (west)

The Verdant Choir is a western federation of forest city-states whose buildings
are trained from living trees and whose major decisions are sung in layered
councils. Large areas of the forest have abruptly fallen silent: no insects, no
birds, no echoing song. Explore consensus and dissent, the occupied ancestral
grove, and the secret expeditions toward the fifth light. Treat the living
forest with ambiguity — it may be intelligent, sacred, ecologically unusual, or
all three, but your chapter must not settle the question.

Visual direction: moss green, black-brown, lichen gold, and muted violet;
growth rings, root networks, woven branches, and sound-wave patterns.

Document form: write the chapter as an annotated score — verses, marginal
dissents, and parts assigned to different voices. This form is yours alone; the
other three realms are using different ones.

### Your two canonical borders

1. **Embersteppe-Verdant:** The Treaty of Ashroot ended a devastating border
   fire 19 years ago. A breakaway Embersteppe war-band now occupies a grove
   that the Verdant Choir considers a living ancestor.
2. **Verdant-Asterfall:** Their high-pass signal towers have begun showing an
   impossible fifth light. Both realms publicly call it a mechanical fault and
   privately send expeditions toward it.
