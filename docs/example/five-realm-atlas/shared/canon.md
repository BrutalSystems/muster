## Immutable world canon

The world is called **The Shattered Meridian**. Four realms surround the Glass
Sea, a circular inland sea created 312 years ago when a fragment of the moon
struck the old imperial heartland. The impact ended the empire, altered local
weather, and left translucent mineral deposits called moon-glass.

An unnatural eclipse will occur in 30 days. Each kingdom has observed a
different warning connected to it, and no realm agrees on what the warning
means. You may not resolve the eclipse, identify an objectively correct
interpretation, or introduce a fifth kingdom. Contradictions between
interpretations are desirable; contradictions in the fixed facts are not.

### Realm positions

- North: Asterfall
- East: Nacre Dominion
- South: Embersteppe Compact
- West: Verdant Choir
- Center: the Glass Sea and its forbidden impact islands

### Fixed border relationships

1. **Asterfall-Nacre:** Asterfall trades stormglass lenses for Nacre
   navigational pearls. A joint caravan disappeared at the Pale Stair eleven
   days ago; each side suspects the other concealed a survivor.
2. **Nacre-Embersteppe:** The receding eastern shoreline has exposed an ancient
   salt road. Nacre claims it by imperial charter, while Embersteppe clans are
   sheltering people displaced from newly stranded port towns.
3. **Embersteppe-Verdant:** The Treaty of Ashroot ended a devastating border
   fire 19 years ago. A breakaway Embersteppe war-band now occupies a grove
   that the Verdant Choir considers a living ancestor.
4. **Verdant-Asterfall:** Their high-pass signal towers have begun showing an
   impossible fifth light. Both realms publicly call it a mechanical fault and
   privately send expeditions toward it.

### Shared mystery constraints

- Every realm possesses one incomplete piece of a pre-impact map.
- Every realm interprets the coming eclipse differently.
- Every realm has a plausible reason to hide something.
- No chapter may establish supernatural claims as indisputable fact.
- References to another realm must use its canonical name and the facts above.
